# Vision and goals

InDubioProAgente is an extensible simulator of rule-governed adversarial and inquisitorial legal proceedings. It combines deterministic execution, autonomous/coupled participant agents and human participation without claiming to replace courts.

## Use cases
1. **Legal education** — practice procedural acts, questioning, deliberation and issue spotting with jurisdiction-specific rules and learning feedback.
2. **Human rehearsal** — voluntary, bounded witness/defendant/counsel participation with graduated pressure and accessibility.
3. **Agentic research** — evaluate institutional cooperation/conflict, information isolation, robustness and inconsistent procedural decisions.
4. **Comparative systems** — German criminal/civil first; later modular other jurisdictions.
5. **Sandbox entertainment** — synthetic cases and configurable traits, clearly distinguished from verified legal simulation.

## Success criteria
A fixture with sealed ground truth and independently controlled perspectives generates a complete, reproducible event trace. Procedural rules can block or annotate violations without sacrificing confidentiality. A later rule pack can re-evaluate the trace without rewriting it. Training feedback can separate communication skills, measured rule conformance and unverified guesses.

## Out of scope for the initial seed
Live agents, realistic adjudication predictions, Germany-wide legal coverage, production authentication, appeals implementation, graphical UI, legal advice and case-specific psychological expertise.
