# Human participant / rehearsal mode

Human-controlled courtroom roles use the same proposal interface as LLM agents. Possible target modes: defendant, witness, advocate, judge, juror or observer where the jurisdiction allows.

## Design commitments
- Consent to simulated role, difficulty and pressure level, with an immediately available pause/stop/skip control.
- Explicit notice that output is synthetic and NOT legal advice, forensic assessment or actual case prediction.
- Adjustable interrogation intensity, pacing, language, accessibility and content warnings.
- Clear distinction between judge-led examination in Germany and adversarial cross-examination where applicable.
- Optional private rehearsal and confidential consultation, never automatically broadcast.
- Training feedback on observable communication/procedure; avoid false certainty about credibility, trauma, guilt or psychiatric status.
- No covert emotional profiling, stress maximization or penalty for exercising silence or other procedural rights.
- Human and agent adapters can be interchanged without special access to sealed evaluator truth.

## Future acceptance tests
During a witness rehearsal, the user can pause immediately, redact a private consultation, exit without publishing the session, and review the exact visible transcript. A human may interject or decline to speak; the engine records the attempted action and jurisdictional consequences without silently suppressing it.
